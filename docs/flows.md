# User flows

## Inspect a recovery case

1. Open the workbench from the hero.
2. Search or filter the synthetic queue.
3. Select a case; focus moves to the updated detail heading.
4. Review signal, owner, runbook, decision guardrail, evidence, timeline, approval state, and metric.
5. Copy a synthetic JSON handoff after an explicit button press.

An empty search hides stale case detail and announces that zero incidents match.

## Review product thinking

Open the collapsed Product thinking disclosure to inspect the persona hypothesis, ranked pains, JTBD, opportunity tree, and riskiest assumption. Hypotheses are labelled; public technical references are not presented as user-research proof.

## Review metrics

The strip and metric tree show synthetic MTTR, human approval coverage, and event-backfill timing. They are modeled demo values, not observed production outcomes.
